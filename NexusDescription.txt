[center][size=4]It finally matters [color=#93c47d]who knocks down heroes[/color] in battle.[/size]
[size=3]Troops [color=#93c47d]lose morale[/color] when their lords, formation captains and generals fall - the other side [color=#93c47d]gains morale[/color] and cheers.
How many troops are affected depends on the role of the fallen hero and of the one who knocked him down.[/size]

[color=#ffff00][size=4]Features[/size][/color]
[color=#ffd966]Troops lose morale when a hero of their side is knocked down in battle.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]The attacking side's troops gain morale.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Generals, formation captains and unassigned heroes count differently - both the one who falls and the one who strikes.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]The attacker's troops cheer when an enemy hero falls.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]On-screen notification when a hero falls, with texts you can change.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#93c47d]Your character shouts a short line when your general or one of your formation captains falls.[/color] ([color=#6aa84f]on[/color]/[color=#e06666]off[/color])
[color=#ffd966]Works in field and siege battles.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]No need to configure anything - every value can still be changed.[/color]
[color=#ffd966]Works with or without Mod Configuration Menu.[/color]
[color=#93c47d]Translations for 12 languages.[/color]

[color=#ffff00][size=4]Who is affected (default settings)[/size][/color]
[color=#6fa8dc]Morale loss[/color] - the fallen hero's side:
General falls - the whole team.
Formation captain falls - the captain's formation.
Unassigned hero falls - troops within [color=#93c47d]10 m[/color] of him ([color=#93c47d]20 m[/color] if a formation captain struck him, [color=#93c47d]30 m[/color] if the general did).
[color=#6fa8dc]Morale gain[/color] - the attacker's side:
General strikes - the whole team (troops within [color=#93c47d]30 m[/color] when an unassigned hero falls).
Formation captain strikes - the whole team for a general, the captain's formation for a captain, troops within [color=#93c47d]20 m[/color] for an unassigned hero.
Unassigned hero strikes - the whole team for a general, troops within [color=#93c47d]25 m[/color] for a captain, [color=#93c47d]10 m[/color] for an unassigned hero.
Troop strikes - the whole team for a general, troops within [color=#93c47d]20 m[/color] for a captain, [color=#93c47d]10 m[/color] for an unassigned hero.
[color=#6fa8dc]Cheering[/color] - not configurable:
General strikes - all troops. Formation captain strikes - his formation.
Unassigned hero strikes - troops within [color=#93c47d]35 / 25 / 15 m[/color] when a general / captain / unassigned hero falls.
Troop strikes - troops within [color=#93c47d]30 / 20 / 10 m[/color] when a general / captain / unassigned hero falls.
